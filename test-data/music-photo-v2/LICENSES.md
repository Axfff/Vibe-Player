The repository's code keeps its existing [root license](https://github.com/Axfff/Vibe-Player/blob/844caf7cd1cfb3348cdd130136763528c3274573/LICENSE.md). These
third-party recordings and photographs **are not relicensed under the code
license**, and project contributors do not become their authors.

- 20 complete recordings: **Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0**. Every official ISRC page
  and its catalogue entry were checked. Original MP3 bytes and embedded tags,
  including album information where present, are retained.
- 31 photographs: **CC0 1.0**. The dedication is the concrete file's license,
  not the generic Commons page or structured-data footer license.
- 2 photographs (`img_0039`, `img_0040`): **CC BY 4.0**, credited individually.

The allowed acquisition list is CC0 1.0, CC BY 3.0, CC BY 4.0. No CC BY-SA,
CC BY-NC, unspecified public-domain assertion, or modern custom stock-site
license is used in this batch. No paid resource or model weight is included.

CC BY 4.0 permits redistribution and modification with appropriate credit,
the license link, and indication of changes. Keep [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md)
with copies and exports. The title, creator, work link, original/downloaded
SHA-256, distributed SHA-256, transformation, and verification date are in
[manifest.json](manifest.json) and the [per-item evidence](evidence/licenses/).
The exact source metadata is retained in those evidence files. CC0 attribution
is provided for provenance even though it is not a copyright condition.

- [CC0 1.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

- [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)

All image candidates use the same JPEG quality and size policy. Metadata was
removed from inference images, with required credit and change notices moved
to the accompanying records. `img_0029`, `img_0030`, and `img_0039` have explicitly
documented crops to remove visible faces or foreground people/horses; these are
not presented as unmodified originals.
The four contact sheets are labelled preview derivatives assembled from these
same images, not additional independent photographs. They carry the same
per-image attribution by neutral IDs through ATTRIBUTION.md.

This selection does not imply endorsement by creators. Individual source pages
were checked on **2026-11-07**; future downloads must match the locked hashes.
If a source changes or licensing becomes unclear, stop and review it in a new
dataset version instead of silently changing the lock.
